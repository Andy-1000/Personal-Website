Chapter 2: Baby Steps

2005 – 2010

From a young age Andy was a precocious, smiley lad, full of curiosity, dairy intolerance and baby fat. As all babies do, Andy began to grow older and larger with each passing year, until finally he decided he needed to do something with his life. That something was primary school.

Chapter 3: Primary School

2011 – 2017

In primary school Andy was a friend to all and an enemy to none. It was here that Andy's love of hitting drums with sticks bloomed. And it was at this age that Andy first began receiving instruction in the word of God, culminating in his baptism.

Chapter 4: High School

2018 – 2023

As a teenager, Andy attended The Gap State High School. Casual work at Officeworks gave him an idea of what it felt like to be rich, while at Teens he learned where his true riches lay. When he wasn't slacking off at school, Andy could usually be found mountain biking, beating his brother at Monopoly, or finessing his latest fun pun cartoon. Throughout his high school years, Andy had to learn how to choose for himself to be in the world but not of the world.

Chapter 5: University

2024 – 2026

Upon completing high school (and quite thoroughly at that), Andy then had to give up his expectations of the season ahead and instead meet the Lord to find his will, which was to attend university. This looked like a double degree in Data Science and Behavioural Economics at QUT. Andy also began work at The Growth Agency, alongside tutoring and other side hustles.

Chapter 6: Today

2026

As of today, Andy is completing his last semester of uni, and completely in faith towards what the Lord has in store for the season ahead. And, there's no better way to mark it than by being surrounded by the people who've been part of his journey. Andy would love for you to join him as he celebrates his 21st!

So, that's Andy.
